## In the Harness

| May flex (the path) | Is fixed (the purpose) |
|---|---|
| Tasks split, merged or reordered | The brief's Goal and success signals |
| A tier raised after a stop | The acceptance criteria, unless the person changes them |
| How many tasks, and how big | The gates: intake, the score review, the eval |

Split freely. Never skip a gate to save time.

When a step is cut, merged or shortened, write two things into that task's section of `PLAN.md`: what the step would have proven, and who accepted the risk of not proving it. That covers a task dropped, a criterion removed, a check deferred, or a task the person finished as it stood. If you can't name both, the step was blurred, not skipped: ask the person.
